A better way to get started

Renting shouldn’t take all your time, money, and energy.

Finding a room or house can mean chasing leads, travelling from place to place, and trying to work out who to contact and what a home costs. EasyRent is built to make the first steps easier: browse available listings, compare the details that matter, and reach out to the person managing the property.

Who we’re here for

For people on the move

Whether you’re finding your first place or filling a vacancy, EasyRent gives you a simpler place to start.

Students

Look for a room or home near campus, within your budget, and suited to how you want to live.

Busy people and families

Short on time or moving to a new area? Narrow the search by location, unit type, and rent range.

Landlords

List units, respond to booking requests, keep tenancy and rent records, and manage your rental activity.

A straightforward process

From search to a conversation

Use EasyRent to organise your search and make contact—then agree on the details directly.

  1. 01

    Browse what’s available

    Explore property listings and view the unit types, rent, location, and details provided.

  2. 02

    Compare your options

    Filter by county, town, unit type, and price. Save listings you want to revisit.

  3. 03

    Contact the landlord directly

    Ask questions, confirm availability, and arrange a viewing with the property contact.

  4. 04

    Make an informed decision

    View the property, verify the details, and agree on rent, deposit, and terms before paying.

One platform, useful tools

More organised for renters. Easier to manage for landlords.

EasyRent helps bring the important parts of a rental search and small property portfolio into one place.

  • Search rentals using location, unit type, and rent range.
  • View listing descriptions, photos, and available unit information.
  • Save places and keep track of booking requests.
  • Manage property units, tenant records, rent payments, and receipts.

Keep your move in your hands

A useful connection—not a substitute for checking.

EasyRent helps renters and landlords find and contact one another. A listing is not a guarantee of identity, ownership, availability, property condition, or agreed terms. Always view the property, confirm who you are dealing with, and get the full costs and agreement in writing before sending money.
